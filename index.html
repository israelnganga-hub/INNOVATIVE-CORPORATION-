What We Build

Three Domains.
One Mission.

INNO CORP operates at the intersection of the world's most urgent and complex challenges. Every system we build is grounded in physics, driven by purpose, and designed to endure.

Sustainable Defence Technology

We are building sustainable, eco-friendly, and highly efficient technology for defense. Advanced engineering systems for national protection that protect lives without harming the world that sustains them.

Environmental & Energy Engineering

We are developing systems that convert captured carbon into usable fuel that can be refined and converted into various forms — a breakthrough solution for clean energy in East Africa and beyond.

Mental Health & Human Relations

Technology in service of human dignity. We are working closely with our partner company JALI to develop mental health diagnostic tools, community connectivity systems, and accessible platforms for underserved communities.
